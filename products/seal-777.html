About this product

Decor Seal 777 has two parts: a specially made polymer liquid and a cement-based powder. When mixed, they form a flexible, durable, waterproof coating that bonds with concrete and masonry.

It holds back high water pressure, stops seepage and protects the structure from moisture, carbonation and weather damage. Use it on new buildings and for repair and renovation.
