About this product

Decor Termi Fix is a Fipronil-based anti-termite solution for long-lasting protection. Use it for soil treatment before construction, treatment after construction, and on wooden structures.

It forms a protective barrier that kills termites at the source and protects buildings and timber from future attacks. The non-repellent formula gives reliable, long-term control for homes, commercial and industrial projects.

How to use

Mix with water as shown in the table. For wooden structures, use with kerosene oil.

Safety: This is a pesticide. Read the label before use, keep it away from children, food and drinking water, and wear gloves.
